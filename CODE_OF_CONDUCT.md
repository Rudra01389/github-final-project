# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Our commitment to this community is to provide a warm, safe, and respectful space for everyone, welcoming individuals of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels with open arms and mutual kindness.

## Our Standards

To maintain a positive atmosphere, we expect everyone to:

- Act with empathy and kindness toward others
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Give and accept constructive feedback graciously
- Own up to mistakes, apologize, and learn from them
- Prioritize the well-being of the community as a whole

We have zero tolerance for:

- Harassment of any kind
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Personal insults, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for protecting this environment. They monitor discussions, review issues fairly and confidentially, and take appropriate and fair corrective action when this Code of Conduct is violated. They have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies everywhere our community interacts, both online and offline. It also applies whenever an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces, such as using an official email address, posting through an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an event.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the community leaders at **your-email@example.com**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and leaders will respect the privacy and security of the person who reported the incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders follow a progressive system of consequences designed to correct behavior while keeping the community safe.

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** A minor mistake, such as unprofessional or unwelcome language.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning with clarity about the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A request for an apology may be made.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** A violation through a single incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A formal warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved is allowed for a specified period of time. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Impact:** A more serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any form of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Impact:** A pattern of violating community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any form of public interaction within the community.
