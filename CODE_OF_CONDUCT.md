Our commitment to this community is to provide a warm, safe, and respectful space for everyone, welcoming individuals of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels with open arms and mutual kindness. To maintain this positive atmosphere, we expect everyone to act with empathy, respect differing viewpoints, give and accept feedback graciously, own up to mistakes, and always prioritize the well-being of the group as a whole. Conversely, we have zero tolerance for harassment, inappropriate sexualized language, personal insults, trolling, sharing anyone's private information without permission, or any other behavior that makes people feel unsafe or unwelcome.

Community leaders are here to protect this environment, meaning they have the responsibility to monitor discussions, review issues fairly and confidentially, and take action when rules are broken. This Code of Conduct applies everywhere our community interacts, both online and offline, as well as whenever anyone is officially representing us in public spaces. If anyone experiences or witnesses bad behavior, they can report it safely knowing their confidentiality will be strictly protected by leadership.

When rules are violated, leaders respond fairly using a progressive system of consequences designed to correct behavior while keeping the community safe. For minor mistakes, a gentle private warning and request for an apology are usually enough. For more serious or repeated violations, leaders may issue formal warnings, impose temporary bans from community channels, or issue permanent bans for chronic harmful behavior. Ultimately, this framework is adapted from the trusted Contributor Covenant to ensure our space remains positive and inclusive for all.
