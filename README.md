A simple interest calculator is a financial tool used to calculate the interest charge or return on a loan or investment over time. The calculation depends on three main factors: the Principal ($P$), which is the starting amount of money; the Rate ($R$), which is the annual interest rate percentage; and the Time ($T$), which is the duration in years. To calculate the simple interest, you multiply the principal, interest rate, and time together, and then divide the result by 100. Written as a formula, this is:$$\text{Simple Interest (SI)} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$Once you have calculated the simple interest, you can find the total final value of the investment or loan by adding the interest earned or owed back to the initial principal. The formula for the Total Amount ($A$) is:$$\text{Total Amount (A)} = P + \text{SI}$$For example, if you deposit a principal ($P$) of $1,000 into a savings account with an annual interest rate ($R$) of 5% for a duration ($T$) of 3 years, you first calculate the interest as $\text{SI} = (1000 \times 5 \times 3) / 100 = 15000 / 100 = \$150$. To find the total amount in your account after 3 years, you add the $150 interest to the original $1,000 principal, giving a total of $1,150.Additionally, if you ever need to calculate a missing variable—such as finding the principal required to reach a specific interest target, or determining the required interest rate—you can rearrange the core formula:To find Principal ($P$): $P = (\text{SI} \times 100) / (R \times T)$To find Interest Rate ($R$): $R = (\text{SI} \times 100) / (P \times T)$To find Time ($T$): $T = (\text{SI} \times 100) / (P \times R)$
